The authoritative prior trace is wifi-ios-scan/ios/backend-full.log (375276B, SHA555765f9ba8a1d46ccb788b7c2c13cddab968b7b1bf9faa0535f8ce8b5aa3563). Failed delivery restores saved scan cursor.next under the client gate; retry yields the same row. Prepared descriptors are completed on every path; no output write on a refused GET.
