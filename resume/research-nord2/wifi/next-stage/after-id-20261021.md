## EMI is a separate unresolved prerequisite

Android does two distinct operations: `emi_mng_init` calls MPU **SET** during driver initialization ([init]($HOME/Documents/Codex/2026-10-19/vedi-questa-repo-https-github-com/work/nord2-current-kernel/drivers/misc/mediatek/connectivity/conninfra/platform/emi_mng.c:235)), giving region27 AP/CONN/SCP access ([policy]($HOME/Documents/Codex/2026-10-19/vedi-questa-repo-https-github-com/work/nord2-current-kernel/drivers/misc/mediatek/connectivity/conninfra/platform/mt6893/mt6893_emi.c:88)). Later cold power-on performs EMI **remapping before D-die/SPI/A-die**, not after CHIP_ID ([order]($HOME/Documents/Codex/2026-10-19/vedi-questa-repo-https-github-com/work/nord2-current-kernel/drivers/misc/mediatek/connectivity/conninfra/platform/consys_hw.c:354)). Neither source policy nor a remap proves inherited effective permission under XNU.

Prior hardware returned constant `a0=2` for all eight structured MPU reads. Selected Linux consumes a0 directly; it has no separate success word. Current evidence cannot identify the shipped monitor handler or determine if2 is register data, another ABI result, or unsupported behavior. Extending to128 queries/all regions now would multiply ambiguous results without fixing semantics. No alternate SMC FID/selector or raw MPU access is justified.

There is no presently justified **bounded** probe that establishes effective AP+CONN EMI permission. An SMC can hang irrespective of query count; an AP EMI read can fault/stall and even successful AP read/write would not prove CONN-domain access or overlap priority. ROM execution is unnecessary to establish policy in principle, but requires a matching shipped BL31 handler contract plus supported current-context metadata covering overlapping regions/priority, reservation and aliases. The existing source tree supplies the caller, not that missing contract. Do not weaken MPU or use ROM execution as a permission test.
